Prepared 2026-10-20 against the current combined engineering candidate. This maps
the six workstreams, acceptance boundaries and delivery requirements in
`NEXT-ASTRA-LARGE.txt`. **Engineering implementation and real operating readiness
are separate. All AI/domain labels remain pending human review.** No source-use
approval, actual pilot, customer evidence, membership change, activation or
external distribution is established by this matrix.

The files and functions below were inspected in the combined checkout. **Final
combined Linux verification passed on Python 3.11.17 and 3.12.14: each runtime
passed all 84 offline scripts and all 343 OMNI test methods across 22 OMNI modules,
plus compilation, export-schema validation, JavaScript syntax and `pip check`.
All 256 selected inputs were unchanged across both runs.** Exact runtime,
command, transcript and input-manifest records are indexed in
[verification/final-combined.json](verification/final-combined.json). Historical
stage receipts still apply only to their recorded hashes. The final tracked-candidate
secret scan and staged/worktree whitespace checks passed; their scope is recorded
in [verification/final-static.json](verification/final-static.json). Patch application, sealed-tree checks and package
hashes are recorded separately in the outer delivery manifest and packaging
receipt. These offline results supply no real pilot,
human-review or operating-readiness evidence.

Status terms used below:

- **Implemented:** a current code path or operator command exists; this says
  nothing about live permission, correctness outside its declared grammar, or a
  final suite result.
- **Fixture-verified:** the cited stage or final combined receipt records bounded
  synthetic behavior for its exact tested snapshot. The current combined
  integration passed on both recorded Linux runtimes; later code/input changes
  need their own verification. This status grants no real pilot or human credit.
- **Researched:** primary documentation or a bounded public source inspection is
  recorded, with retrieval date, fields and limitations. Research grants no rights.
- **Blocked:** a specific missing contract or unsupported representation prevents
  that broader behavior; refusal, unknowns or coverage debt remain explicit.
- **Requires real/operator evidence:** the importer/validator may exist, but its
  actual source, reviewer, custody, timing, costs, consent or approval inputs are
  absent. A synthetic attestation cannot satisfy this status.
