## What is now implemented

### 1. Durable source lifecycle, origins and coverage

Source changes append immutable observations bound to exact source/native
identity, old and new revisions, metadata/evidence hashes, versions and affected
events. Newly learned metadata, discrepancies, corrections, amendments and
authoritative withdrawals remain distinct. Material changes put claims into a
durable quarantine independent of hydration/job state. A restart, cached body,
replayed observation or old approval cannot clear it.

Review validates the current event, source state, policy and implementation.
Packets, queues, history, validation and companion output withhold stale approval.
Transactions and idempotent writes preserve earlier receipts, rejected and
superseded states, and resumed exhibit batches. Clinical definition documents are
stored as separate dependencies: a change to a definition can quarantine its
dependent result without confusing an old definition date with result freshness.

Reviewed correspondence distinguishes copies, republication, independent reports,
corrections and retractions using exact revisions and source proof. A transitive
copy chain represents one assertion origin. Similar headlines, tickers and dates
do not establish correspondence or independence. Reads revalidate imported proof;
a generic receipt envelope cannot bypass that rule.

Feed observations, acquired index sets and correction surveillance now have
separate receipts. Feed windows use the prior actual acquisition clock only when
route, family, query, policy, implementation and captured traversal match. Source
publication time is not substituted for observation time. Caps, missing bodies,
outages and deferred work retain explicit debt. SEC full/quarterly comparisons
bind the exact quarter, reject rollover comparisons and record missing entries as
discrepancies until authoritative evidence supports withdrawal. All six formerly
design-only origin/lifecycle fixtures now execute.

### 2. Scoped clinical definitions and amount atoms

The clinical implementation accepts optional versioned endpoint, treatment and
cohort definitions only with exact evidence and disclosure-time applicability.
Validator checks preserve trial, phase, arm, population, analysis and endpoint
rank. A catalog mapping has its own source permissions and correction lifecycle;
it is never installed as human-approved research truth by this assignment.

The bounded parser represents supported effect measures and exact evidence spans,
including constrained table headers/cells, confidence intervals, p-values and
adjustment context. The 41 retained numeric rules and both supplied parser repairs
remain covered. Twenty-two additional clinical forms test ambiguity and refusal.
General HTML/PDF tables, inherited footnotes/units, censored or changing risk sets,
unsupported safety transfers and unrepresented statistical adjustments abstain.
Primary outcomes sharing an unresolved trial scope may remain contested; exact
distinct endpoint/analysis evidence is required to separate them.

Scoped amount atoms retain decimal text, currency, operator, role, contract or
financing identity, period, contingency, additivity, revision and field proof.
Ceilings, included/incremental options, obligations, authority and gross/net
proceeds remain separate. Bounds, ranges and approximations keep their kind.
All 12 original amount scenarios have declared outcomes, alongside 28 additional
fixtures. Existing exact-USD behavior remains protected. The implementation does
not infer conversion, annualization, revenue, received cash or runway. Resumed
filing exhibits regenerate the full numeric and amount projection.

### 3. Prospective evaluation and evidence intake

The main CLI now supports freeze, source-first enumeration, closure,
adjudication, timing/coverage import and evaluation. A future protocol binds
sampling, source/route/family strata, window, versions, event units, registry and
query plan before observations. An independently acquired source inventory defines
recall. A separate complete inventory of emitted proposals defines precision.
Unknown issuers, misses, rejected/non-events and missing adjudications remain
visible rather than disappearing from denominators.

Historical audit, development, calibration and prospective/untouched partitions
remain separate. Exact event-family correspondence, current source proofs and
deduplicated assertion units prevent copied or reused events inflating results.
Downstream reports rederive protocol, enumeration, inventory, run and adjudication
evidence even after generic receipt imports. Invented cycles, omitted unknowns,
stale versions and cross-family matches cannot create credit.

Timing uses actual source availability, observation/acquisition/review clocks,
precision, uncertainty and referenced intervals. A supplied latency scalar or
truthy continuous-source flag is insufficient. Untouched-test tooling includes
prespecification, sealed manifests, custody/access records, controlled evaluation,
consumption, contamination and implementation-drift invalidation. Software records
do not authenticate a human or prove that an external person never saw the data.
The original release thresholds remain unchanged and automatic activation stays
false. Synthetic sessions receive no real pilot credit.

### 4. Disabled NRC, EIA and procurement adapters

Verified narrow read-only contracts support pure parsers and explicit collectors.
Transport validates method, route, query/body, purpose, credential reference and
attempt budget before a request; it retains existing GET safeguards, bounded
responses, backoff and redaction. All 20 proposed vertical scenarios now execute,
with additional caps, outage, replay, permission and identity controls.

NRC support preserves accession/docket/project identity, licensing-stage
distinctions and separate document/addition/observation clocks. Unknown timezones
stay unknown and metadata leads precede downloads. EIA support separates pinned
route metadata from responses, generator/plant identity, owners from operators,
dated stakes, annual vintages and revised periods. The inspected EIA-860 workbook
layout has a bounded OOXML parser; unverified vintages and incomplete fallback
inventories are refused. NRC-derived lineage is retained across duplicate hosting.

Procurement support separately represents delayed award aggregates and transaction
obligation changes, modifications, action dates and update/deletion uncertainty.
Unknown currency or publication time remains unknown. Neither an award total nor
a transaction delta becomes issuer revenue or a real-time award claim.

No NRC/EIA key, subscription or account was obtained. Current live route metadata,
exact approvals, mutable-pagination completeness and project/legal-entity/issuer
crosswalks still require evidence. These gaps are documented without enabling
routes or inventing payload fields.

### 5. Measured expansion proposals

Expansion uses explicit operator candidate lists and set difference against
versioned current coverage. Baseline/candidate arms bind exact issuer/security
evidence, source policy, implementation, windows and query scope. Collector hooks
validate the frozen measurement context before transport and preserve the complete
native inventory; classifier discoveries do not define the acquisition sample.

Reports calculate deduplicated useful-event yield, mapping errors, unknown-issuer
and negative-outcome recall, abstention, backlog, requests/retries, review effort
and aligned incremental costs from referenced records. Missing or mismatched
windows, confounding changes, sparse/correlated samples and missing actuals remain
explicit. Zero useful events has no finite per-event cost. Typed Wave A/candidate
review evidence replaces truthy placeholders, with human authentication limits
stated. Reports only propose eligibility; they do not create a Wave B membership
list, fan out collectors or change signal eligibility.

### 6. Rights, reviewed briefs, economics and dependent gates

Rights decisions bind exact source/native revision, fields, purpose, audience,
transformation, versions, attestation, expiry and revocation. Denial prevails and
missing rights withhold fields. Brief export regenerates the permitted current
projection and validates its complete content, including semantic proof fields
and historical publication inspection. Nested lead and ingest diagnostics cannot
leak unapproved source spans. JSON, Markdown and HTML exports use new local files;
rendered source text is escaped.

Typed intake covers review effort, complete operating cost inventories,
useful-brief assessments and consented feedback, including negative feedback.
Economics preserves exact decimals, signed nonlabor credits, duplicate external
IDs, currency, reporting windows, complete categories and aligned useful units.
Unknown inputs remain unknown; templates create no actuals. Typed OMNI-10/11/12
reports validate exact protocol, source/candidate, version and operating-window
dependencies. Pending, stale, synthetic or incomplete records keep gates pending.

The runbooks cover actual command dispatch, private input locations, backup and
restore, correction incidents, first authorized acquisition and review. They leave
actual deployment and approval work with the operator.
